# Hi, I'm Miha 👋

I’m an iOS developer working on [Lake](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1183717726), a coloring app that won an [Apple Design Award](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2017/06/apple-design-awards-celebrate-the-best-in-innovation-and-creativity/). I enjoy a good challenge, especially when it takes a bit of detective work.

Outside work, I prefer nature to cities, and [Goni Pony](https://www.redbull.com/si-sl/events/goni-pony/goni-pony-eng-info) is my kind of day out. It’s a race up Slovenia’s highest road pass on tiny retro bikes with no gears. So far, two finishes and one broken bike. I share a home with my wife, our kid, four Pony bikes and two cats who mostly tolerate me. Happy to chat over coffee, or preferably a good beer.
